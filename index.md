GLIC brings together software, databases, technical knowledge and community expertise to support research involving glycans and glycoconjugates. It is also a meeting point for glycoscientists and glycoinformaticians.

GLIC is a community, not a service provider: most of the software and databases listed here are developed and maintained by their own teams, many of them GLIC members.
