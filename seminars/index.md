# GLIC Seminar Series

The aim of the GLIC seminar series is to introduce and promote software tools and databases to the bioinformatics and (glyco-)biology community.

## Seminar series

* [Special Topic: Glycan Arrays](glycan-arrays-2023/index.md) (February–March 2023): six webinars on tools and databases for processing, interpreting and storing glycan array data.
