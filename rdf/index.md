# GLIC RDF endpoint

GLIC manages a SPARQL endpoint for glycan-related RDF (linked) data. The data sets loaded into it, their licences and their graph names are listed in the GLIC RDF repository on GitLab.

* [GLIC SPARQL endpoint](https://sparql.glycoinfo.org/sparql): run SPARQL queries in your browser or from your own software.
* [GLIC RDF Support (GitLab)](https://gitlab.com/glycoinfo/glic/rdf): the data sets, their licences and graph names.

**Availability:** the endpoint answered a test query when the website maintainers last checked it, on 22 November 2026. It is not monitored continuously. If it does not respond, please tell us on the [GLIC mailing list](https://groups.google.com/g/glycoinformatics-consortium-glic).
